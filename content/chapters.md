[[extra.faq]]
q = "Do we have to be a political party?"
a = "No. Many chapters will start as an association or a think tank and only later decide whether to stand in elections. What matters is that the group is independent, democratic inside and open about its money."

[[extra.faq]]
q = "Can we change the programme?"
a = "Yes, that is the point. The manifesto is shared; the programme is yours. Most measures on this site are written so they can be adapted to other institutions."

[[extra.faq]]
q = "Who may use the name and the globe mark?"
a = "The texts are free to use under CC BY 4.0. The name The Future, the wordmark and the globe mark are not covered by that licence: a chapter uses them once it has been founded and agreed with us, so the name keeps meaning the same thing everywhere."

[[extra.faq]]
q = "Does it cost anything?"
a = "No. There is no fee to start or run a chapter."
