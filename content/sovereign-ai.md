Without its own computing capacity and its own models, a country depends on a few foreign providers: on their prices, their rules and their off switches. What most countries lack is not talent but a strategy that turns research computing and open models into public infrastructure. In Switzerland, for example, the Alps supercomputer and the open language model Apertus give the Swiss chapter a concrete starting point."
