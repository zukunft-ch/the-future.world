# Progress for everyone

> Prosperity, comfort and well-being for all — with a modern social contract that creates security and expands freedom.

Source: https://the-future.world/

- [Sovereign AI & compute](https://the-future.world/sovereign-ai/): Compute is infrastructure: public capacity, open models, and AI we can audit, run and replace ourselves.
- [Basic income](https://the-future.world/basic-income/): The automation dividend as a stable foundation: simpler welfare, freedom to retrain, tested in pilots.
- [Work without fear](https://the-future.world/transitions/): Retraining and career changes as the norm: fewer hurdles, skills over diplomas, learning alongside work.
- [Digital sovereignty](https://the-future.world/digital-sovereignty/): Critical systems under democratic control: no vendor lock-in, open standards, secure supply chains.
- [Re-industrialisation](https://the-future.world/robotics/): Automated production close to home, in factories, fields and building sites, and affordable for small firms.
- [Health with trust](https://the-future.world/life-science/): Medical progress with consent: health data opt-in, evidence-based rules, no black-box medicine.
- [Cities & housing](https://the-future.world/cities-housing/): Affordable homes and short commutes by building upward and inward at transit hubs, not outward.
- [Research & commons](https://the-future.world/research-innovation/): Testbeds, open standards and public return on public funding, so innovation benefits everyone.
- [Resilient infrastructure](https://the-future.world/energy-resilience/): Power, networks and supply that keep working: reliability as the metric, fewer critical dependencies.
- [Open government](https://the-future.world/open-government/): Transparent by law, data via APIs, offices filled on merit: a state citizens can check and build on.
- [Peace & diplomacy](https://the-future.world/peace-neutrality/): Mediation, dialogue and a strictly defensive stance: strong at home first, credible abroad.
- [Talent & rule of law](https://the-future.world/talent-integration/): Open to people who want to build something, with clear rules that apply to everyone.
- [Language & understanding](https://the-future.world/language-understanding/): Local languages stay; a shared bridge language makes cooperation, work and integration easier.
