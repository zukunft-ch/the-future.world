# Privacy Policy

> Privacy policy of The Future.

Source: https://the-future.world/privacy/

## Privacy policy

### Responsible party

The Future
Email: [hey@die-zukunft.ch](mailto:hey@die-zukunft.ch)

### Principle

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We only collect data that is necessary for the operation of this website and our work.

### Contact form and chapter enquiries

If you use the contact form or the chapter enquiry form, we process your name, email address, country and message to answer you. The form is handled by our own small application on Cloudflare infrastructure, shared with our Swiss chapter Die Zukunft (api.die-zukunft.ch). Your message is stored for at most one year and is not shared with third parties. Your IP address is used only briefly to prevent abuse and is not stored with your message. You can also simply email us.

### Newsletter

If you subscribe, we store your email address and, if you give it, your country, for at most one year after subscribing. We use them only to send updates about The Future and its chapters. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any update or emailing us.

### Display preference

If you switch between light and dark mode or pin sections, the website stores that in your browser’s local storage (keys `dz-theme` and `dz-pins-v1`). It is not sent to us. The website also caches public pages and assets in your browser to make repeat visits more reliable. You can remove this data in your browser’s site settings.

### Hosting

This website is statically hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. No cookies are set and no tracking tools are used. Fonts, scripts and images are served from this domain; the only other address the site talks to is the form handler named above, and only when you submit a form.

### Your rights

You have the right at any time to request information about your stored data, its correction or deletion. Please contact us at the address above.

### Changes

We reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy as needed. The current version can always be found on this page.
