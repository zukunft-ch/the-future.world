# Program

> Our program for structural transformation.

Source: https://the-future.world/program/

- [Sovereign AI & Compute](https://the-future.world/sovereign-ai/): Compute is the infrastructure of the 21st century. Every society needs its own capacity, open models and the ability to audit and operate AI itself.
- [Basic Income](https://the-future.world/basic-income/): Basic income as the automation dividend: stability, freedom, simplification.
- [Work Without Fear](https://the-future.world/transitions/): Career changes, learning, fresh starts as the norm — not the exception.
- [Digital Sovereignty](https://the-future.world/digital-sovereignty/): Sovereignty starts with infrastructure: open, auditable, portable — and secure.
- [Re-industrialisation with Robotics](https://the-future.world/robotics/): More production and value creation close to home: automated, local, open to small businesses. In the field, on the building site and in the factory.
- [Life Science & Health](https://the-future.world/life-science/): Enabling progress — with transparency, consent and control.
- [Cities & Housing](https://the-future.world/cities-housing/): Affordable housing and quality of life through densification at transit hubs — less sprawl, more comfort.
- [Research, Innovation & Commons](https://the-future.world/research-innovation/): The best place for responsible innovation — transparent, interoperable, internationally compatible.
- [Energy & Infrastructure Resilience](https://the-future.world/energy-resilience/): Reliable, affordable and independent infrastructure as the foundation for prosperity and sovereignty.
- [Open Government](https://the-future.world/open-government/): A state that enables rather than blocks: transparent by law, data and procedures via APIs, offices filled on competence rather than party membership.
- [Peace & Diplomacy](https://the-future.world/peace-neutrality/): Diplomacy, mediation and de-escalation as strategic strengths — strong at home first, then credible abroad.
- [Talent, Integration & Rule of Law](https://the-future.world/talent-integration/): Openness for talent and clear rules — a hub for people who want to contribute.
- [Language & Understanding](https://the-future.world/language-understanding/): A model for clear communication, strong diversity and integration without friction.

## Our program for transformation

Our program addresses the central challenges of structural change — pragmatic, measurable and adaptable to local conditions worldwide.
