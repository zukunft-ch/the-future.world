# Talent, Integration & Rule of Law

> Openness for talent and clear rules — a hub for people who want to contribute.

Source: https://the-future.world/talent-integration/

## Talent, Integration & Rule of Law

Thriving societies are built on stability, quality and trust — and on people who contribute. We want to strengthen both: **openness for talent** and **clear rules** that apply to everyone.

## A Hub for International Talent

We want people from around the world to be able to research, found companies, work and invest — and for the places that welcome them to become stronger as a result. This is part of our prosperity model. Concretely: a talent visa for outstanding people in AI, robotics, life sciences and research, with a decision within days rather than months, family included and relocation made simple.

## Integration Means Commitment

Those who move to a new country should not just "be there", but **arrive there**:

- Learn the language and orient themselves
- Access to work and education
- Participation in social life

## Rule of Law and Security

Rules are the foundation of trust. Clear, fair, without hysteria:

- Expectations are clear and apply to everyone
- Violations are consistently enforced under the rule of law
- No generalisations based on origin — responsibility is individual

## Why This Fits The Future

Abundance and comfort emerge in a society that is **open** to capability and commitment — and **reliable** in its rules. Exactly this combination makes societies strong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you want more or less migration?

That's the wrong question. We want countries to be open to people who contribute — researchers, founders, skilled professionals — while staying reliable in their rules. The quality of integration is what counts, not symbolic debates.

### What does 'integration means commitment' mean?

Those who move to a new country should truly arrive: learn the language, have access to work and training, take part in social life. It is both an expectation and an offer.

### And those who don't follow the rules?

Rules apply to everyone, and violations are consistently prosecuted under the rule of law. But responsibility is individual — no generalisations by origin.

### Why is talent part of the prosperity model?

Because prosperity emerges where skill and effort are welcome. People who research, found and invest in a place make it stronger — and everyone benefits.
