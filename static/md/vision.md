# Vision

> Artificial intelligence and robotics are changing what work is and where prosperity comes from. The Future is a movement that shapes this transition: with compute, open AI, talent and production close to home, and with a dividend that reaches everyone.

Source: https://the-future.world/vision/

When machines do cognitive and physical work, prosperity for everyone becomes possible. We make sure communities build it instead of renting it.

## What is happening right now

Artificial intelligence makes cognitive work cheaper, robotics makes physical work cheaper. It is the biggest economic shift since industrialisation, and most institutions are less prepared for it than they need to be.

### 2047: General AI is getting closer

In the largest survey of 2,778 AI researchers, the probability that machines will do every task better than humans by 2047 was put at 50 percent. That is 13 years earlier than in the previous survey. Nobody knows when. We plan for the case that it comes early.

Source: [Grace et al., JAIR](https://jair.org/index.php/jair/article/view/19087)

### 1,220: Automation is uneven

Robots per 10,000 industrial workers in South Korea. Germany has 449, Switzerland 294. Places that automate keep and win back production. Places that hesitate lose it.

Source: [IFR World Robotics 2025](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas)

### Talent: The race for people has started

Singapore, Hong Kong and the United Kingdom run dedicated visas to attract top researchers and founders. Talent goes where it is welcome, quickly and with its family.

Source: [Singapore ONE Pass, Hong Kong TTPS, UK Global Talent](https://www.gov.uk/global-talent)

Open alternatives already exist. One example: with **Apertus**, ETH Zurich, EPFL and CSCS released one of the world's most open large language models in 2025, trained on the **Alps** supercomputer in Lugano. Public institutions can build, not only buy. What is usually missing is the political will to turn this into a programme.

## A place in 2040

Not science fiction, but what any city, region or country can achieve with today's technology and political will.

### Dealing with the authorities in minutes

Taxes, permits, moving house: the state handles routine cases automatically, with reasons given and a right of appeal. The people in public administration have time for the difficult cases.

### An AI assistant for everyone

Everyone has access to a trustworthy AI assistant in their own language, for health, law, education and everyday life. On infrastructure the community controls, with strong data protection.

### The factories come back

Medicines, precision parts, electronics and food are made in highly automated plants close to where people live. Shorter supply chains, less dependence, new work in planning, maintenance and development.

### Building in half the time

Prefabrication, construction robotics and digital permits bring homes to market faster and more cheaply. More housing is a question of productivity, not of going without.

### Farms with robots

Autonomous machines weed, harvest and tend crops with precision. Farms stay productive and independent, even as fewer people work in agriculture.

### More time, less existential anxiety

The gains from automation flow back to everyone as a dividend: a foundation that makes retraining, starting a business and care work possible.

## The abundance stack

Prosperity from AI does not come from an app. It comes from layers that build on each other. If one is missing, the whole thing does not hold. That is why we think them through together, from energy to the dividend.

### Energy

No electricity, no compute and no robots. Affordable, reliable supply in every season, with the extra demand from data centres and automation planned in from the start.

### Compute

Data centres and AI capacity close to home, accessible to universities, small businesses and public administration. If you only rent compute, you rent your future.

### Sovereign AI

Open models in local languages with transparent data. For critical applications the rule is: models we can audit, operate and replace.

### Talent

Train, keep and attract the best minds, with fast, welcoming visas for people who come to build research, companies and whole industries. Continuing education as the norm.

### Application in every sector

Robots in fields, on building sites and in factories. AI in every service and in public administration. Wherever it takes load off people and keeps value creation close to home.

### Dividend

The productivity gains reach everyone: through a basic income as a foundation, safe transitions between occupations and lower everyday costs.

## Three sectors, one goal

Machines should work where they take load off people and keep value creation close to home. In every sector of the economy.

### Precision instead of drudgery

Agriculture is losing workers in many countries. Robotics keeps farms productive and food supply close to home.

- Autonomous weeding, tending and harvesting robots
- Shared machinery pools for small and medium-sized farms
- Sensors for precise use of water and fertiliser
- Automated processing close to the farm

### Production comes back

Highly automated manufacturing makes local production competitive again, even where wages are high. Local means: short distances, less dependence, know-how that stays.

- Highly automated plants for medtech, pharma, precision and electronics
- Shared robotics centres for small businesses
- Construction robotics and industrial prefabrication
- Being able to make critical goods close to home

### Every service gets better

AI makes services faster, cheaper and more accessible. The state leads the way and becomes the best service provider around.

- Administration: transparent by law, routine cases automated, every decision explained
- Offices filled on competence, with term limits
- AI assistance in health, law and education
- Lower costs in banking, insurance and retail
- More time for the personal, the complex and the caring

## Fast, but under control

People adopt new technology when they can trust it. That is why clear rules apply for us.

### Human responsibility.

Every decision about people has a responsible person, a stated reason and a right of appeal.

### Auditability.

AI in public use is documented, tested and independently auditable.

### Privacy by design.

Health and personal data only with consent. Critical processing under local control.

### Safety before scale.

Powerful systems are tested for risks before they are deployed in critical areas.

### Freedom of choice.

Personal access to public services remains guaranteed for everyone who needs it.

## Why small places can go first

### Real-world laboratories

Cities and regions can test what national governments only debate. What works in one place, others can adopt. A network learns faster than one big centre.

### Open by default

Methods, code and results are published, so a pilot in one place becomes a blueprint for the next.

### Trust through participation

Change that people help decide is change they carry. Democratic legitimacy is a speed advantage, not a brake.

### Build on strengths

Every place has something: a university, an industry, cheap clean power, a skilled workforce. Automation makes these strengths bigger, not smaller.

### Learning across borders

Local chapters share tools and experience. One example: the Swiss chapter, Die Zukunft, publishes its programme and research openly.

## A movement for this transition.

Established politics argues about how today's pie is divided. AI appears as a risk, as a regulatory question, or not at all. Yet the next ten years will decide who owns tomorrow's value creation.

**The Future** puts this question at the centre. We understand the technology, we take its risks seriously, and we have a plan: compute and open models close to home, the best talent, local production and a dividend for everyone.

Not left, not right. Forward.

## What we push for

- **2028: Open AI models in use in public administration.** Operated on infrastructure the public controls, in local languages, auditable and replaceable.
- **2028: Basic income pilots under way.** With independent evaluation of bureaucracy costs, business start-ups and well-being.
- **2028: Fast, welcoming talent visas.** For researchers, founders and specialists in AI, robotics and life sciences: a decision within days, family included.
- **2030: Every automated decision about people explained and appealable.** A stated reason, a responsible person and a right of appeal, by law.
- **2030: Publicly accessible AI computing capacity.** For universities, small businesses and public administration, with the electricity demand planned in from the start.
